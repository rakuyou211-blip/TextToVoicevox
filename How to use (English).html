TextToVoicevox ・ Windows

From install to read-aloud

An app that pulls the text out of PDFs, images and text files and reads it aloud in VOICEVOX voices such as Zundamon. Everything runs on your own computer; your text is never sent anywhere. You can also open this page from 使い方 (how to use) at the top right of the app.

The app's screen is in Japanese only. Button names below are written in Japanese exactly as they appear in the app, with a short English gloss.

VOICEVOX→Python→The app→ Add files→Extract text→Pick a voice→Make audio

Setup (first time only)

Install in this order. VOICEVOX and Python are separate programs and are not included in the app.

  1. 1

    Install VOICEVOX

    VOICEVOX is the program that actually makes the voices. Download and install it from the official VOICEVOX site.

    • After that there is nothing else to do. Opening the app also starts the speech engine (no editor window; it closes together with the app).
    • If you also want the editor window, start it with the app's VOICEVOX起動 (start VOICEVOX) button.
    • To stop it starting by itself, untick アプリと一緒に VOICEVOX を起動 (start VOICEVOX with the app) under 接続設定… (connection settings) at the bottom left.
    • If your PC has a GPU and your VOICEVOX is the GPU build, the app starts it on the GPU, which makes audio much faster (about 4× on the PC we measured). To turn this off, untick GPU があれば使う (use the GPU if there is one) in the same place.
  2. 2

    Install Python

    Download the installer for Python 3.9 or newer from python.org and run it.

    The most common stumbling point

    On the installer's first screen, be sure to tick “Add python.exe to PATH” before you continue.

  3. 3

    Start the app

    Double-click 起動.pyw in the extracted folder.

    • The first time only, a black window runs the setup (needs an internet connection; takes a few minutes).
    • When it finishes, the app opens. From then on it opens right away.
    • After the first start, a “TextToVoicevox” shortcut with the app’s icon appears, and 起動.pyw moves into the app folder. Open the shortcut from then on.
    • When a new version is out, “新しい版があります” appears at the top. Click it to see how to update.
    • Always right-click the zip → “Extract All” before using it.

    Another way: paste one line into Terminal (no Python needed, no warning, and you get an icon on your desktop)

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rakuyou211-blip/TextToVoicevox/main/install.ps1 | iex

How to use it (every time)

Add a file, extract the text, turn it into speech. These four steps are the basics.

  1. 1

    Left column, ① 取り込む

    Add what you want read

    Drag and drop a PDF, an image (PNG, JPG and so on), or a .txt, .docx or .epub file onto the window.

    • To read text that is on your screen, press スクリーンショット (screenshot, Ctrl+R) and drag a box around it. It is read aloud right away.
  2. 2

    Left column ① 取り込む → the text on the right

    Extract the text

    As soon as you add a file, its text is extracted into the text area on the right. To extract again, press ▶ テキスト抽出 実行 (Ctrl+Enter).

    • For PDFs you can set a page range such as 5-320 to skip the cover and table of contents (設定 ▷ in ①).
    • In PDF books, the book or chapter name printed at the top or bottom of every page (the running head) and the page numbers are not read aloud; the first occurrence of a chapter heading (第一章 and so on) stays. You can see what was taken out under 直した所 (fixes).
    • Scans and photos are read too (vertical text from the right column), including text over game scenes and photos, small text, and Japanese mixed with English.
  3. 3

    The text on the right / left column ② 声

    Fix the text and pick a voice

    You can edit the text directly. The furigana above the kanji show how VOICEVOX will actually read them, so you can spot misreadings before you listen (click a colored one to fix that word's reading). In ② pick a character and a voice style, then check it with ▶ 試聴 (Ctrl+P).

    • Furigana appear only while VOICEVOX is running. If it is not, a line right above the text says so (「ふりがな（漢字の上の読み）は、VOICEVOX を起動すると出ます」), and the 画面なしで起動 button next to it starts VOICEVOX. In a narrow window the furigana checkbox sits below the text.
    • Tick 「誤読候補だけ」 (likely misreadings only) to show just the colored readings.
    • In a long book, 次の候補 ▷ (next candidate, F8; Shift+F8 goes back) at the top right of the text jumps to each colored reading in turn. Lines whose readings haven't been looked up yet are checked with VOICEVOX as it goes.
    • The text grows when you widen the window (in a narrow one it never shrinks below your size). Set your own size with Ctrl++ / − (or Ctrl + mouse wheel) and go back with Ctrl+0. Right-click → 「文字の大きさ」 (text size) turns the automatic sizing on or off.
    • Start a line with @ずんだもん: to switch the voice for that line only (for dialogue).
    • Lines that start with # are not read (use them for notes).
    • You can also fix a misreading by selecting the word, right-clicking, and adding it to the pronunciation dictionary.
    • Dates, times and symbols (25℃, 10:30, 2026/11/7, 10〜20人 and so on) are rewritten before speaking so that VOICEVOX reads them right; your text stays as it is. To turn this off, untick 数字・日付・記号の読み方を直す at the top of the 読み方辞書... (pronunciation dictionary) window.
  4. 4

    The player bar under the text / left column ③ 保存

    Listen, or save it as audio files

    • ▶ 再生 (right under the text) … reads from the line with the cursor to the end. Space pauses and resumes. The line being read stays about a third of the way down the text, so you can see the next sentence. The player bar shows the line number and the time left (あと約◯分).
    • ◁ 広げる (widen, F11, at the right end of the player bar) … folds the left column so the text fills the window, and the text grows with it. ▷ 戻す (back), or Esc when nothing is playing, brings it back.
    • ♪ 音声を生成 (Ctrl+G) … saves files in the format chosen in ③ (WAV and others). By default everything goes into one file; change that with まとめ方 (how to split) in ③. If a save would make more than 300 files, the app asks first. Tick 字幕(.srt)も保存 to get subtitles too.
    • 段落・章は長め (longer pauses at paragraphs and chapters; next to 文の間 in ③, on by default) … leaves a longer pause where a new paragraph starts (after a blank line or a full-width-space indent) and before chapter headings such as 第一章 (1.0 s and 2.0 s when the sentence gap is 0.4 s), both when playing and in saved audio. Paragraphs indented with a full-width space are found even with 空行を削除 (remove blank lines) on.
    • Press Esc to stop midway. You can also save what has been made so far.

If something goes wrong

Double-clicking 起動.pyw does nothing, or Notepad opens

Python is not installed, or the PATH box was not ticked. Redo setup step 2. If Python is installed but Notepad opens, right-click → “Open with” → choose “Python”.

“Windows protected your PC” appears

Click the small “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The author simply has not code-signed it; all of the source code is public.

「VOICEVOX: つながっていません」 (not connected to VOICEVOX) appears

VOICEVOX is not running. If it is installed, it normally starts when you open the app. If it didn't, press VOICEVOX起動 or 画面なしで起動 at the bottom left of the app (接続, connection). If this happens every time, check that アプリと一緒に VOICEVOX を起動 (start VOICEVOX with the app) under 接続設定… at the bottom left is on.

No furigana appear

Furigana appear only while the app is connected to VOICEVOX (until then a line right above the text says so, with a 画面なしで起動 button next to it). Start VOICEVOX, or press VOICEVOX起動 or 画面なしで起動 at the bottom left of the app; they appear by themselves once it connects. In a narrow window, the furigana checkbox sits below the text.

You can't tell what's wrong

Start the app with デバッグ起動.pyw to see the error in a black window. Records are also kept in the folder: 起動エラー.log and エラー.log (if you installed from the zip, all of these are in the app folder).

When you publish audio you made, follow the terms of VOICEVOX and of each character, and add a credit such as “VOICEVOX:ずんだもん”. The button on the completion screen copies it for you.
